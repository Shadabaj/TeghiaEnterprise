## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-precision, tactile, and authoritative digital presence tailored for luxury turnkey interior execution, structural refits, and civil engineering consultancy. The brand aesthetic balances the raw permanence of stone, slate, and steel with the refined nuance of boutique architectural millwork and bespoke interior staging.

### Brand Personality & Core Virtues
- **Architectural Gravitas:** Grounded, unshakeable, and structurally integral. The interface projects the permanence of concrete slab and cast bronze rather than fleeting digital ephemera.
- **Craftsmanship & Materiality:** Focuses heavily on textures, natural stones, patinated metallics, and pristine dimensional tolerances.
- **Executive Reliability:** Communicates absolute fiscal and schedule precision for high-net-worth homeowners, commercial project directors, and corporate procurement executives.

### Visual Style Model
A tailored synthesis of **Warm Architectural Minimalism** and **Tactile Tonal Layering**. The interface avoids stark digital coldness by utilizing warm ivory substrates, structural graphite framing, hairline divider rules mimicking architectural section cuts, and deliberate accents of burnished terracotta bronze. Visual weight is communicated through balanced typographic hierarchy and proportioned layout grids rather than heavy simulated shadows or ornamental skeuomorphism.

## Colors

The palette directly references architectural materiality: raw slate, honed limestone, weathered terracotta, and cast bronze.

### Palette Implementation
- **Primary (`#1E2228` — Basalt Charcoal):** Anchors high-priority structural elements, heavy display typography, navigation frames, and primary actions. It serves as our anchor tone, carrying structural authority without the stark harshness of synthetic pure black.
- **Secondary (`#B86B35` — Burnished Terracotta Bronze):** Used with strict discipline for focused calls-to-action, active stage markers, status milestones, highlighted structural metrics, and precision accents. It should never exceed 10–15% of total viewport real estate.
- **Tertiary (`#4A5568` — Concrete Slate):** Deployed for sub-headers, technical data labels, auxiliary badges, and structural architectural diagrams.
- **Neutral Surface System (`#FBF9F5` — Stone Ivory):** Forms the foundational canvas, paired with `#F0ECE1` for surface tiers and `#E2E8F0` for structural gridlines, dimensional dividers, and panel outlines.

### Functional Roles & Contrast Standards
- Text hierarchy must preserve contrast ratios above 7:1 for all primary editorial text on ivory backgrounds.
- Secondary bronze elements must rely on deep charcoal (`#1E2228`) text when applied as a solid fill to guarantee legibility, or appear as solid tone strokes over light surfaces.

## Typography

The pairing reconciles the classical authority of architectural manifests (`Newsreader`) with the pristine clarity of structural schematics (`Hanken Grotesk`).

### Editorial Hierarchy Rules
- **Display & Headlines (`Newsreader`):** Used for architectural statements, narrative lead-ins, section themes, and quote marks. It features a calibrated optical size and subtle editorial italic variations for portfolio project locations and material specifications.
- **Body & Precision Data (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Delivers clean readability across project management dashboards, civil specification tables, material bill-of-quantities, and general narrative prose.
- **Micro-Labels & Technical Captions:** Rendered in `label-sm` or `label-md` with uppercase transformation and expanded tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mirror technical architectural blueprints and dimensional callouts.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an architectural modular grid reminiscent of drafting paper and construction drawings.

### Grid Anatomy & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid locked inside a max-width container of 1440px. Structural margins sit at `3rem` with `1.5rem` gutters. Elements span structural increments (3, 4, 6, 8, 12 columns).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Multi-column editorial blocks reflow into stacked paired modules.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer canvas padding and `1rem` column gutters. Architectural photography cards collapse into full-width edge-to-edge stacks.

### Rhythm & Alignment
Spacing reflects spatial balance. Vertical cadence between major project chapters or services uses `space-3xl` (`6rem`), internal module clusters use `space-xl` (`2.5rem`), and tight component internals use `space-sm` to `space-md`. Thin 1px structural gridlines (`#E2E8F0`) may visually anchor columns to emphasize architectural drafting precision.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid synthetic, overly digital UI tropes, this design system rejects blurry, heavy drop-shadows. Depth is derived from **tonal layering**, **structural surface shifting**, and **razor-thin perimeter rules**.

### Surface Stratification
- **Floor Surface (Base):** `#FBF9F5` (Stone Ivory) acts as the unyielding base slab.
- **Plinth Surface (Level 1 Cards & Structural Containers):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or pale limestone tint (`#F6F3EC`) bordered by a crisp 1px rule of `#E2E8F0`.
- **Raised Module (Level 2 Drawers, Dialogs, Floating Navigation):** Crisp solid `#FFFFFF` elevated solely by an ultra-diffused, ambient architectural ambient shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(30, 34, 40, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 34, 40, 0.04)`.
- **Inverted Core (Inspection Overlays, Technical Nav):** Solid Basalt Charcoal (`#1E2228`) with hairline boundary strokes of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy mirrors stone fabrication, planed timber, and structural joinery: predominantly crisp corners softened only by an artisanal bevel or subtle easing.

### Edge Treatment
- Base elements (buttons, inputs, status pills, panel cards) utilize `0.25rem` (`roundedness: 1`), removing raw digital sharpness without becoming round or playful.
- Modals, large architectural gallery viewports, and primary structural cards scale up to `0.5rem` maximum.
- Never use fully rounded pill buttons (`9999px`) or hyper-curved bubbly forms; structural integrity requires orthogonal confidence.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action:** Solid Basalt Charcoal (`#1E2228`) container, warm stone text (`#FBF9F5`), `0.25rem` radius. Padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Typography: `label-lg`. On hover: smooth transition to Terracotta Bronze (`#B86B35`).
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with a 1px border of `#1E2228`, Charcoal text. On hover: shifts to `#F0ECE1` fill with no border repositioning.
- **Accent Architectural Action:** Solid Terracotta Bronze (`#B86B35`) fill with Deep Charcoal text (`#1E2228`) for critical conversion checkpoints (e.g., "Request Site Inspection", "Download BOQ Specification").

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Set against white or stone-ivory surfaces with a 1px border in `#CBD5E1`. Internal padding: `0.75rem 1rem`. Typography: `body-md`.
- **Focus State:** 1.5px solid border in `#1E2228` accompanied by an uppercase floating micro-label in `#B86B35`. No default browser glow.
- Numeric inputs for square footage and material estimates incorporate trailing architectural units (e.g., `SQ.FT.`, `INR`, `MM`) styled in `label-sm` slate text.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- White base, framed by a continuous 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
- Composed of three vertical tiers: an edge-to-edge 16:10 ratio architectural photograph, a metadata band showing project location ("Sector 20, Kharghar") and turnkey category, followed by a bold editorial headline in `Newsreader`.

### Chips & Material Tags
- Height: `28px`. Background: `#F0ECE1`. Text: `#4A5568`. Border: 1px solid `rgba(74, 85, 104, 0.15)`. Font: `label-sm` with letter-spacing of `0.06em`.
- Active or Selected State: Solid `#1E2228` with `#FBF9F5` text.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Box size: `18px x 18px`. Border: 1.5px solid `#4A5568`. Corner radius: `2px` for checkboxes; full circular for radios.
- Checked State: Solid `#1E2228` fill hosting an architectural geometric checkmark or inner core in `#B86B35`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Bill of Quantities (BOQ) Table:** Crisp zebra-free tables delineated solely by horizontal 1px lines (`#E2E8F0`). Headers set in `label-md` uppercase slate. Numerical columns align right using tabular figures.
- **Renovation Phase Tracker:** Linear horizontal milestone bar with etched square nodes (`8px x 8px`) denoting milestones (Demolition, Masonry, MEP Rough-Ins, Millwork, Handover) connecting via 2px slate and bronze conduits.
